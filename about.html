About OneTick

OneTick helps you clear your mind.

Increase your productivity and master time management. We believe a to-do list should feel calm, not crowded.

Our story

Simple on the surface. Powerful underneath.

Most to-do apps make you choose: simple but limited, or powerful but overwhelming. OneTick was built to be both.

On the surface you get a clean, comfortable interface that gives you a clear glance at everything you need to do. Underneath, smart categories, colour-coded priorities, due dates, reminders and private lists are ready the moment you need them. Familiar iOS gestures and fluid animations tie it all together, so getting organized never feels like work.

OneTick is designed and built in Causeway Bay, Hong Kong, and shaped by feedback from the people who use it every day.

What we believe

Three words guide every decision.

Minimalist.

Every screen has to earn its place. Fewer taps, less clutter and more room to think.

Productive.

Features exist to help you finish things, from smart categories to reminders that arrive right on time.

Powerful.

Beneath the calm surface: priorities, sorting, search, task details and privacy locks whenever you want them.

How it works

From busy mind to clear plan in three steps.

  1. CaptureJot down whatever is on your mind the moment it arrives. It takes seconds.
  2. OrganizeAdd a priority, a due date or a few details. Smart categories sort the rest.
  3. TickGet reminded at the right time, tick it off, and enjoy the feeling of a clearer day.
