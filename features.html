Features

A new level of task management.

Customisable to-do lists at your fingertips, priorities and due dates that keep you on track, and smart categories that keep everything organized.

Categories Lists Priorities Due dates Task details Search & sort Privacy lock And more
Smart Categories

Your day, sorted before you ask.

Four smart categories on the Home screen group similar tasks together automatically, each with a progress bar so you can see how far you've come.

  • Today: everything due today, in one focused view.
  • All: every task across every list.
  • Starred: the tasks you've marked as important.
  • Planned: anything with a due date, lined up in order.
Lists

A list for every part of life.

Create lists for work, groceries, movies to watch or places to eat, and add an emoji so you can spot each one instantly. Every list shows how many tasks are left.

  • Create a new list in a single tap from the Home screen.
  • See dates, times and priority stars on every task.
  • Tick tasks off with a satisfying, fluid animation.
Priorities

Know what matters most, at a glance.

Every task can carry one of five colour-coded priority levels. Sort a list by importance and the urgent work rises straight to the top.

Urgent High Medium Low None
Due dates & reminders

Plan ahead. Never be late.

Pick a date from the built-in calendar, set a time, and OneTick takes care of the rest. Turn on notifications and you'll be reminded before every deadline.

  • Set a due date and time while you create a task.
  • Dated tasks appear in Planned automatically.
  • Check upcoming schedules, events and appointments in seconds.
Task details

Add details. Never miss an event.

Some tasks need more than a title. Add exactly what you need, and nothing you don't.

  • Due date and time for anything with a deadline.
  • Location so you know where to be.
  • Description for notes, links and context.

Search & sort

Find anything in a flick.

Speed is the key to productivity. Search any list as you type, sort it the way you think, and hide what's already done.

  • Instant search results as you type.
  • Sort by due date or importance.
  • Hide or show completed tasks with one tap.
Privacy lock

Private lists stay private.

Lock any list behind Face ID or your iPhone passcode. Hand your phone to a friend without worrying about what they'll see.

  • Lock and unlock lists with a single tap.
  • Authentication is handled securely by iOS.
  • OneTick never sees your Face ID data or passcode.

Help for the hard part: actually starting.

A list tells you what to do. These two help you do it, and remember where you left things.
