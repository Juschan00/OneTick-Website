    FAQ

    Questions? Answered.

    What is OneTick?

    OneTick is a minimalist to-do list for iPhone. It brings your tasks, lists, due dates, priorities and reminders together in one calm, fast app, so you can organize tasks, plan your day and visualize ideas in one place.

    Which devices does OneTick support?

    OneTick is designed for iPhone and takes full advantage of iOS gestures, notifications and Face ID.

    Can I keep some lists private?

    Yes. Lock any list and OneTick will ask for Face ID or your iPhone passcode before showing it. Authentication is handled by iOS, so OneTick never sees your biometric data.

    How do I get help or suggest a feature?

    We'd love to hear from you. Visit our support page or email support@onetick.com.
